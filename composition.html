Active Health Goals & Protocol

Primary Goal: Lose body fat slowly while preserving vital muscle tissue, especially because you are taking Mounjaro and insulin.

  • Protein Target: 90–110 g/day spread across meals to support muscle preservation and recovery.
  • Resistance Training: Perform compound exercises 2–3 times per week to trigger muscle protein synthesis.
  • Weight Targets: Focus on steady progress. First target is 155–160 lb; Second target is 145–150 lb.

Important Protocol Rules

Clothing Adjustment: Scans include clothes (jeans/t-shirts), accounting for ~5–5.5 lb difference compared to home morning weight. Never directly compare clothed scan weight to morning home weight.

Future Scans: Retest every 8–12 weeks. Always wear the same light gym clothes (t-shirt and shorts) to standardise clothed scans.
